Studio Guidelines

  • No Food or Drinks: Please keep food and open cups outside. Bottled water is fine!
  • No Smoking/Vaping: Severamente vietato fumare in tutte le sale per proteggere le attrezzature e i pannelli fonoassorbenti.
  • Time is strictly enforced: Your setup and pack-down time must be included in your booked hours.
  • Leave it as you found it: Rimetti i cavi, i microfoni e le aste al loro posto a fine sessione.
